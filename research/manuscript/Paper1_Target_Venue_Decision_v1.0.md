## Decision

### Primary target — Robotics and Autonomous Systems (RAS)

RAS is the strongest fit for the current manuscript. Its scope explicitly covers theoretical, computational, and experimental aspects of autonomous systems or modules of such systems. The fit is reinforced by recent publication of the closely related ADAMAS ontology+planning mission-management paper and multiple heterogeneous mission-planning papers in RAS.

The submission framing should remain:

> **mission-conditioned heterogeneous Provider–Action domain membership as an explicit, auditable governance boundary, evaluated directly with same-cardinality controls and a held-out native-semantic external gate.**

### Second target — IEEE Transactions on Automation Science and Engineering (T-ASE)

T-ASE is methodologically compatible through system modeling, planning/scheduling/coordination, risk management, and intelligent automation. Adaptation cost is higher: IEEE double-column format, <=200-word Regular Paper abstract, a 100–300 word Note to Practitioners, and tighter page discipline.

### Third target — Journal of Intelligent & Robotic Systems (JIRS)

JIRS is a credible fallback with recent bounded-autonomy, context-model, planning/validation, and UAV-autonomy work. RAS nevertheless provides the more direct semantic-planning lineage.

## RAS adaptation priorities

1. Compress the ~400-word v0.4.3 Abstract to 230–280 words.
2. Make the opening read as an autonomous mission-planning architecture paper, not a defense-application or generic AI-governance paper.

7. Do not change experiments or conclusions for venue fit.
